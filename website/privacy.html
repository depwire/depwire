Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Who controls your data

ATEF ATAYA LLC is the data controller for the Depwire announcement list.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address, IP address, browser user agent, the timestamp of your consent, and the source of the signup.

Why we collect it

We use this information to send release and major feature announcements and to retain a record of how consent was given.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare provides site hosting, Workers, the D1 database, and Turnstile bot protection. Turnstile is used to block automated submissions.
  • Resend provides email delivery.

Unsubscribing

Every announcement email contains an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing marks your record inactive and stops all further mail.

Bounces and complaints

Email addresses that hard-bounce or report an announcement as spam are automatically deactivated.

Access, correction, and deletion

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, email atef@depwire.dev.
